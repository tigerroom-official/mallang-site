삭제 요청 방법

  1. tigerroom.official@gmail.com으로 제목 "말랑픽 계정 삭제 요청" 메일을 보냅니다.

  • 계정(로그인) 정보: 게스트 계정 식별자, 연결한 Google 계정 정보

삭제하면 복구할 수 없고, 남은 유료 티켓도 함께 사라집니다.

보관 후 파기하는 정보

  • 이용 통계(Firebase Analytics): 최대 14개월 뒤 자동 삭제됩니다.

기기 안의 기록은 앱을 삭제하면 지워집니다. 자세한 내용은 개인정보처리방침을 참고해 주세요.

Malang Pick Account Deletion

Tiger Room · Malang Pick

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email tigerroom.official@gmail.com with the subject "Malang Pick account deletion".
  2. Include the email you linked with Google, or the player number (PID) shown at the bottom of the title screen.
  3. After confirming it is you, we delete the data within 30 days and reply.

What Is Deleted

  • Account (sign-in) information: the guest account identifier and the linked Google account information
  • Game progress and ticket balance stored on our server

Deletion cannot be undone, and any remaining paid tickets are lost.

What Is Kept for a Period

  • Purchase records: kept separately for 5 years as required by the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce of Korea, then deleted.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): deleted automatically after at most 14 months.

Progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. See the Privacy Policy for details.
